About

Hi. My full name is Le Hong Phuc. You can call me Phil if "Phuc" sounds weird to you. I am originally from Vinh City. I studied and lived in Hanoi for around 10 years and currently, I'm working and living in Ho Chi Minh City.
I have been working in the industry as a 2D artist for around 6 to 7 years. Most of my knowledge comes from self-study, tutorials, books, short-term painting and drawing courses, as well as valuable help and feedback from my colleagues.
My workflow involves using multiple software, ranging from 2D software like Adobe Photoshop and Adobe Illustrator to 3D software like Maya, Blender, Zbrush, Substance Painter, and a bit of Unity.
I also use AI tools such as Midjourney, Stable Diffusion, ChatGPT, and Vizcom when needed at work to help create products and meet deadlines.
